# Aisha · GTM System Engineer

I'll take on the responsibility of building every system your revenue engine needs, from the moment a potential buyer is identified to when they become customers or subscribers, and beyond.

I work across three layers of go-to-market:

1. **GTM systems (AI agents, workflows, Python, SQL, n8n, Clay, CRMs and others):** the systems that run the full customer journey across sales, marketing, product and customer support.

2. **Outbound systems:** end-to-end outbound: buying signals, list building and enrichment, ICP segmentation, copywriting, and sending infrastructure with healthy deliverability.

3. **RevOps:** CRM administration, integration, and reporting (HubSpot, Salesforce, Attio).

## How I work

1. **Meet with each revenue team.** Understand how they work and identify friction and forces.
2. **Map the process.** Find the best strategy to remove or reduce friction for the teams and clients.
3. **Build the system.** Connect the tools, data, workflows, and AI agents end to end.
4. **Ship it live.** Deploy it so the team uses it, then measure the results.
5. **Plan for what breaks.** Check limits, costs, and failure points up front, so the system holds up on the hard days.
